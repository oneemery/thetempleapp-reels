# You Are In Barbados (58s, 1080x1920, watercolor)

Logline: Neville wants to go home but has nothing, so he lives in the end until the world catches up; then the same move is handed to you.
World: one winter New York night that warms into Barbados. Palette arc: indigo and snow blue (0 to 21s) to gold ticket light (17 to 29s) to warm paper and coral for the teaching (29 to 51s) back to the snowy window, now glowing from inside (51 to 58s).
Motif: the bed and the window. Cold window in shot C becomes a sea window; the ending returns to the same window from outside, glowing.
Characters: Neville = slate blue body, fedora. Abdullah = ochre body, top hat, bigger, calm.
Arc: sad, hopeful, confused, sleepy and content, excited, crushed then starstruck, playful, determined, relieved, transformed (gold), content.

Shots (voice times):
A 0.0 to 7.8 [in: fade from paper] snow falls on a street; Neville on a stoop under a lamp turns out empty pockets (sad, sweat); on "Barbados" a painted dream cloud with palm and sun rises (hopeful).
B 7.8 to 12.6 [brush wipe] Abdullah's lamp lit room; Neville worried; on "you are in Barbados" Abdullah points and the window behind floods warm with a palm (Neville surprised, then ?).
C 12.6 to 17.9 [match on window] Neville's cold room, snow window; he lies in bed, eyes close on "asleep"; on "already in his bed back home" the room washes turquoise and peach, window becomes sea and palm, zzz.
D 17.9 to 21.7 [iris on envelope] morning door; envelope arcs through the slot on "letter", he opens it, the ticket glows gold on "ticket" (excited).
E 21.7 to 29.9 [whip to dock] dock and steamship; grey ticket on "third class" (crushed); Abdullah's face in a warm cloud on "first class"; on "sailed" the ticket flares gold, horn steam, Neville jumps, ship sails right.
F 29.9 to 34.7 [brush wipe] warm paper stage close up; "same move in a different costume": hats swap on each beat while Neville repeats the identical little point move.
G 34.7 to 37.4 [cut on action] a grey rain cloud over a grey paper (the fact); on "off" he turns his back on it and the cloud shrinks.
H 37.4 to 41.3 [pan right] a warm doorway glowing with the Barbados scene; on "done" it opens fully; on "feel it real now" he steps toward it, hearts.
I 41.3 to 47.4 [carry] at the doorway with a suitcase: on "visit once" he peeks and backs out (cross pop); on "live from it" he sets the suitcase down, hangs the fedora; on "slept in Barbados" a hammock under the palm, zzz.
J 47.4 to 51.9 [fade through gold] study with a glowing open book and candle; on "transformed" his body warms from blue to gold, sparkles on "renewing".
K 51.9 to 58.5 [brush wipe] the opening street at night again, pushing into his window: inside it glows like Barbados; he sleeps smiling. Iris closes on the window after "end".

Captions: word by word, three or four words at a time, current word in gold, lower third (y 1300).
